Password Tips

✅ DO

  • Use at least 12 characters
  • Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols
  • Use unique passwords
  • Consider passphrases (e.g., Coffee!Runs4Miles#)

❌ DON'T

  • Use dictionary words (e.g., "password")
  • Use personal information (birthdays, names)
  • Use simple patterns (12345, qwerty)
  • Reuse passwords everywhere
